Business Proposal

This proposal covers the first two phases of your foreclosure data product: Phase 0 to lock scope and business rules, and Phase 1 to build the internal MVP for Step 4 in your workflow, the data upload/update process currently handled through Google Drive Excel 2 to 4 times per week.

Important Scope Note

This proposal does not assume GoHighLevel or any specific CRM. Your workflow shows a future CRM/database step, but the current starting point is your Excel/Google Drive process. In Phase 0, we confirm the best data hub platform before anything is built.

The goal is not to automate all 14 steps at once. The goal is to turn the highest-value manual data work into a structured, repeatable system that can support future automation, skip tracing, and subscriptions later.

1. Business goal and MVP focus

Based on the workflow diagram and our discovery calls, your business has two potential sellable data products:

  1. Product 1, foreclosure and tax lien data: up-to-date Bay Area Peninsula opportunities, focused on properties with strong equity, especially above $500,000.
  2. Product 2, future skip-traced owner data: owner names and phone numbers for high-equity opportunities, likely as a later premium tier or add-on.

The MVP quoted here focuses only on Product 1 operations and the Step 4 data hub. Product 2, skip tracing, and client subscriptions are not included in this proposal.

Phase 1 is a manual-in, structured-data-out MVP. It will make your current research easier to update, verify, filter, and eventually sell. It will not automatically scrape every county, trustee, auction, or skip-trace source in this first phase.

Two lookup workflows are important, but they are intentionally future automation:

  1. Trustee/auction-status lookup (Step 6, with Step 7 checks): use TS number, trustee name, and source links to verify the current auction status across trustee, attorney, and foreclosure-posting websites. Phase 1 only stores and tracks these fields; it does not automate the lookups.
  2. Property-owner contact lookup (Step 5 skip tracing): use owner name, property address, and related property fields to enrich records with phone numbers, emails, and relatives. This can become a later premium data tier; it is not included in Phases 0–1.

3. Business rules to confirm in Phase 0

Phase 0 will turn your current know-how into written rules before build starts. The working rule set includes:

  1. County coverage: San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, and any Bay Area Peninsula counties you confirm.
  2. Source tracking: county recorder data, trustee/company pages, attorney/posting websites, auction information, and manual verification notes.
  3. Status logic: PPD/postponed, AF/aged foreclosure, new auction, first auction vs. second auction.
  4. Auction math: first auction minimum bid logic, second/aged foreclosure balance logic, and any trustee-specific exceptions.
  5. Financial logic: estimated market value, unpaid balance, estimated equity/profit, 15% estimated transaction/documentation cost, and ROI.
  6. Product filters: equity above $500,000 for the main data product; future lower threshold or skip-trace product can be scoped later.
  7. Validation rules: missing TS number, county, auction date, source link, trustee, address, or status should be flagged before publishing.

  6. Social media, Lucie, Google, outbound sales automation, or homeowner call scripts.
  7. Legal/compliance advice, data resale rights review, or guarantee of data accuracy/revenue.
Why this matters

The value of this project is the quality and freshness of the data. Phase 1 protects that value by creating a reliable internal system first. Once the workflow is working with real data, later phases can safely automate more of the collection, verification, skip tracing, and subscription delivery.
